# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 29, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Runly collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website and services.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you create or use a Runly account, we may receive information such as your name, email address, account identifiers, authentication information, subscription status, and account settings.

When you use Runly, we may also process prompts, project files, code, AI conversations, commands, logs, workspace information, usage statistics, and information required to operate development environments.

We may collect technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, timestamps, error logs, and security-related activity.

## 2. Payment Information

Payments may be processed by independent payment providers.

Runly generally does not directly store complete payment card details.

Payment providers may provide us with information such as transaction identifiers, payment status, subscription status, billing country, and limited payment information required for billing and fraud prevention.

## 3. Authentication

Runly may allow users to sign in through third-party authentication providers such as Google or GitHub.

When you use these methods, we receive the information necessary to identify your account and complete authentication.

The exact information provided depends on the permissions you approve with that provider.

## 4. GitHub and Repository Data

If you connect GitHub or another repository provider, Runly may access repositories or account information according to the permissions you authorize.

This access may allow Runly to read files, generate code, modify project files, create commits, manage branches, or perform other actions requested by you.

You may revoke third-party access through the relevant provider where supported.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use information to operate Runly, authenticate users, provide AI functionality, process subscriptions, maintain projects, operate sandboxes, improve reliability, prevent abuse, provide customer support, troubleshoot problems, and develop new features.

We may also use limited analytics to understand how people interact with Runly and improve the service.

## 6. AI Processing

Information you submit to Runly may be processed by AI systems and infrastructure providers when necessary to generate responses or perform requested development actions.

Runly may use third-party technology providers to provide parts of the service.

We aim to share only information reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality.

## 7. Service Providers

Runly may work with service providers for areas including hosting, databases, authentication, payment processing, code hosting, analytics, security, email, and AI processing.

These providers may process information on our behalf according to their own agreements, policies, and applicable laws.

## 8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Runly may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences, improve security, analyze website performance, and provide platform functionality.

Some cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly.

## 9. Security

Runly uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting your password, connected accounts, API credentials, repositories, and other sensitive information associated with your account.

## 10. API Keys and Secrets

Users should avoid placing private API keys, passwords, credentials, or other secrets directly into prompts or public project files unless the platform specifically provides a secure method for storing them.

Where Runly provides secret-management features, users should use those features instead of committing sensitive credentials into source code.

## 11. Data Retention

Runly may retain account and service information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Certain temporary development environments, logs, generated files, or cached information may be removed automatically.

## 12. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Runly account through available account controls or official support channels.

Some records may need to be retained where required for fraud prevention, accounting, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or legitimate security purposes.

Deleting a Runly account does not necessarily delete information stored independently with third-party providers such as GitHub or payment processors.

## 13. Sharing of Information

Runly does not sell users' personal information as a normal part of operating the service.

Information may be shared with service providers where needed to operate Runly, with authorities when legally required, during a business restructuring or acquisition, or when necessary to protect Runly, its users, or others from serious abuse or security threats.

## 14. International Processing

Runly and its service providers may process information in countries different from the country where you live.

Data protection laws may differ between jurisdictions.

Where required, appropriate protections may be used for international transfers.

## 15. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights relating to your personal information.

These may include requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or information about how your data is processed.

Requests may be submitted through Runly's official support channels.

Runly may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

## 16. Children's Privacy

Runly is not intended for users below the minimum age required to independently consent to online services under applicable law.

Where parental or guardian permission is legally required, users must obtain that permission before using Runly.

## 17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Runly changes.

The latest version will be posted on our website and will display its most recent revision date.

## 18. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be submitted through Runly's official support channels.
