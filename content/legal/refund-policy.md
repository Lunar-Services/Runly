# Refund Policy

**Last updated: September 29, 2026**

This Refund Policy explains when purchases made through Runly may qualify for a refund.

## 1. Subscription Refunds

Runly subscriptions provide immediate access to digital services and computing resources.

Because these resources can begin being consumed immediately after purchase, subscription payments are generally non-refundable once substantial usage has occurred.

For a first-time subscription purchase, you may request a refund within **7 days of purchase** if the account has used less than **10% of the included usage allowance** for that billing period.

Meeting these requirements does not affect any additional refund rights you may have under applicable consumer law.

## 2. Subscription Renewals

If a subscription automatically renews and you did not intend to continue it, you may contact Runly within **48 hours of the renewal charge**.

A refund may be issued where there has been no meaningful use of the renewed subscription.

Requests made after significant usage has occurred may be denied.

## 3. Used AI Credits or Tokens

AI credits, tokens, compute allowances, or other usage-based resources that have already been consumed cannot normally be refunded.

Unused allowances do not have cash value and cannot normally be transferred to another account.

## 4. Additional Usage Purchases

Purchases of additional tokens, credits, compute time, or similar usage resources are generally non-refundable once any portion of the purchased resource has been used.

If a purchase was made accidentally and remains completely unused, contact support as soon as possible.

## 5. Duplicate Charges

If Runly or its payment provider accidentally charges you more than once for the same purchase, the duplicate transaction will be eligible for correction or refund after verification.

## 6. Technical Problems

If a serious technical issue caused by Runly prevents you from using a paid service for a significant period, contact support.

Depending on the situation, Runly may provide replacement usage, service credit, an extension, or a refund where appropriate.

Short outages, scheduled maintenance, third-party disruptions, or issues caused by the user's device, network, code, repository, or configuration do not automatically qualify for a refund.

## 7. Beta Products

Certain beta, preview, or early-access products may have limitations or incomplete functionality.

Where a product is clearly identified as beta before purchase, ordinary beta limitations do not automatically qualify for a refund.

This does not remove refund rights required by applicable law.

## 8. Account Suspension

Users whose accounts are suspended or terminated for serious violations of the Runly Terms of Service are not automatically entitled to a refund.

Runly will still comply with refund obligations required by applicable law.

## 9. Chargebacks

Before opening a payment dispute or chargeback, we encourage users to contact Runly support so we can investigate the issue.

Fraudulent or abusive chargebacks may result in account restrictions or suspension.

Opening a legitimate payment dispute does not remove any rights provided by applicable law.

## 10. How to Request a Refund

Refund requests should be submitted through Runly's official support system.

Include the email connected to your Runly account, the transaction or invoice information, the purchase date, and a short explanation of the issue.

Approved refunds will normally be returned to the original payment method.

Processing time after approval may depend on the payment provider or bank.

## 11. Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy limits consumer rights that cannot legally be waived.

Where local law provides stronger cancellation or refund rights than this policy, the applicable legal requirements will take priority.

## 12. Contact

Refund requests and billing questions can be submitted through Runly's official support channels.
